# Training curriculum

These training specs are intentionally ordered.

1. `01`-`04`: direct navigation to different win targets.
2. `05`-`07`: alternate `__ is you` rules plus longer wall mazes.
3. `08`-`18`: broken-rule reconstruction, pushable blockers, and a small amount of defeat pressure.
4. `19`-`20`: unwinnable examples for negative experience.

All `.level.txt` files in this folder compile into numeric maps with `build_levels.py`.
